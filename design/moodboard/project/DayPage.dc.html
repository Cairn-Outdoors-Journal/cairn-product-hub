The Day

Left Reds Meadow before the store opened, five days of food in the pack. The climb out was dust and switchbacks, then the lakes: Rosalie, Shadow, Garnet, each a colder blue than the last. Hops slipped at the Shadow Creek crossing and laughed about it for a mile. Reached camp with an hour of light. Banner Peak turned orange, and then all at once it didn't.
