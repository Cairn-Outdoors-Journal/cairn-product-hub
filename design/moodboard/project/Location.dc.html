Where you were

Each time you open Cairn or capture something, it can save one location point. Points give each day a dotted route, an elevation, and the weather for where you actually were.

  • One point when you open Cairn or capture something. Never continuous tracking.
  • Kept in your journal, encrypted. Never sold or shared.
  • Delete any point, or pause for a day, from the page margin.
